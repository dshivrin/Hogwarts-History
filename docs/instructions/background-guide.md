# Background Guide

Read this only for canon, era-boundary, or placement ambiguity.

## Project Purpose

Build an evidence-backed seed for a fan edition of `Hogwarts: A History`. The project indexes source evidence; it does not draft final book prose.

## Core Principle

Do not invent facts. Every entry must be traceable to a source location. Use short quotes only and prefer paraphrase in notes.

## Historical Boundary

Working assumption: the original in-universe book predates Harry's Hogwarts years, with an approximate planning cutoff up to 1984 unless revised later.

- Older Hogwarts facts may belong in the original book body.
- Harry-era scenes may confirm older features.
- Harry-era events usually belong only in later editorial notes.

## Inclusion Priorities

Include evidence when it is:

- Explicitly attributed to `Hogwarts: A History`.
- Explicitly attributed to another in-universe book, class, teacher, ghost, portrait, or school tradition.
- A concrete Hogwarts setting, rule, custom, magical system, or historical detail.
- A recurring practice or likely future chapter/section seed.

Exclude weak plot movement, unsupported inference, and character drama with no institutional value.

## Candidate Parts

Common candidate parts include:

- Origins of the School
- The Castle and Its Grounds
- Magical Architecture and Enchantments
- The Four Houses
- Ceremonies and School Traditions
- Academic Life and Curriculum
- Rules, Discipline, and Governance
- Ghosts, Portraits, and Magical Residents
- Protective Magic and Security
- Quidditch and School Recreation
- The Library, Books, and Scholarship
- Notable Events Before 1984
- Later Editorial Notes
- Appendix: Explicit References to Hogwarts: A History
