# Unified Expanded Edition — Legacy-Path Editorial Policy

This file operationalises `authoring/editorial-policy.md` for material retained
under the legacy `authoring/editions/1984/` path. The directory name is not an
evidence cutoff.

The historian is an informed participant in the magical world's documentary
tradition, not an omniscient narrator. Canonical truth and historically available
knowledge are related but not identical.

## Initial principles

1. Canonical evidence may establish an event without proving that the
   reconstructed narrator had access to it.
2. Later evidence may establish an earlier event, origin, or persistence for the
   unified edition; its claims and authority still require direct review.
3. Private conversations should not automatically become historical facts
   available to the narrator.
4. Legendary material should remain legendary where documentary support is
   absent.
5. Contradictory historical testimony may be presented historiographically.
6. A convincing reconstruction is preferable to false certainty.
7. Evidence gaps should be acknowledged rather than filled with invented lore.
8. Not every available evidence entry needs to appear in the manuscript.
9. Duplicate records should normally support one synthesized claim rather than
   produce repeated prose.
10. Historical significance determines space, not evidence-entry count.
11. Each chapter dossier must record event chronology and knowledge access as
    separate fields.
12. Outlines must label reconstructed Bagshot passages and later editorial
    additions. Separate addenda are reserved for material that would confuse
    chronology, perspective, or reading flow.
13. Evidence must not be rejected solely because its source or subject postdates
    1984.
14. For each new numbered manuscript revision `NN`, evidence must be
    consolidated and validated in `evidence-synthesis-revision-NN.md` before
    `outline-revision-NN.md` is created; both must correspond to the resulting
    manuscript revision.
15. Generic `outline.md`, generic `draft.md`, and outline material embedded in
    preparation packages remain valid historical artifacts. When revision `NN`
    is active, its exact matching revisioned synthesis and outline take
    precedence and generic material must not silently substitute for either.
16. Approved manuscripts and unapproved working comparison baselines are
    distinct. Using a working revision for comparison does not approve it or
    replace the approved manuscript pointer.

## Open editorial decision

The final citation and footnote system is not yet defined. Evidence IDs must be
preserved in chapter working materials in the meantime so that any later
citation apparatus remains traceable to the canonical research records.
