## Strict Chapter 18 reuse check

- The pantomime is used for staff overlap and a cross-tenure consequence, not
  as another demonstration of abstract headmasterial authority.
- The 1943 section gives Dippet the formal decision and Dumbledore suspicion and
  advocacy. It does not repeat Chapter 18's general distinction between office
  and influence as a constitutional survey.
- The Lupin material is used as one chronological and personal case. Tunnel
  geography, the Willow's containment function and the general scope of
  headmasterial discretion remain outside Chapter 20.
- The Trelawney appointment is compared with Hagrid and Lupin only after the
  three different mechanisms are stated. The comparison remains a pattern of
  individual judgement, not a new legal power or inclusion policy.

## Book-wide chronology and terminology

- Names and offices are consistent: Armando Dippet, Albus Dumbledore, Rubeus
  Hagrid, Remus Lupin, Sybill Trelawney, Headmaster, Transfiguration and Defence
  Against the Dark Arts.
- The sequence is consistent: undated Dippet-era pantomime; 1938 Riddle visit;
  1943 Chamber crisis; relatively dated first teaching request; 1945
  Grindelwald victory; undocumented succession; later teaching request to
  Headmaster Dumbledore; Lupin and Trelawney decisions; First Wizarding War
  through 1981; 1984 boundary.
- Dippet's accession, departure and successor-selection remain undocumented.
  The 1945 victory is not made an accession date or cause.
- Dumbledore's suspicion is not assigned to Dippet or the wider staff. Riddle's
  later confession is not used to judge Dippet as though it were contemporary
  information.
- Hagrid's retention remains Dippet's formal act following Dumbledore's
  advocacy.
- Lupin, Hagrid and Trelawney remain distinct cases rather than a general
  programme.
- Wartime reputation remains distinct from defences, emergency policy and
  guaranteed safety.
- No post-1984 event or later Dumbledore history is imported merely to complete
  the final chapter.

## Section and cross-chapter overlap

- Repeated facts are compressed to the amount required for their new function.
  The chapter does not re-teach admissions, pupil festivity, the Hogsmeade
  route, Chamber mechanics, headmasterial powers or modern institutional
  pressure.
- The closest overlap is with Chapter 18 because both Dippet and Dumbledore
  appear there. Chapter 20's new value is chronological: it establishes their
  long overlap, preserves the missing succession and follows mixed consequences
  from one administration into the other.
- The 1945 achievement recurs from earlier chapters only to separate public
  stature from accession and then to explain later wartime standing.
- No Chapter 20 sections require merging. Each movement advances a distinct
  stage in the institutional chronology before the final synthesis.
