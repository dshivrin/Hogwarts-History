## Chapter Purpose

Show how repeated entry rites, Great Hall gatherings, seasonal observances and
House competition gave Hogwarts a recognisable institutional year, while
documented experiments, crises and exceptional ceremonies demonstrate that
continuity depended on adaptation rather than identical repetition. The chapter
begins at Chapter 12's shift from memory to custom and ends at the threshold of
Chapter 14's fuller account of Quidditch culture.
