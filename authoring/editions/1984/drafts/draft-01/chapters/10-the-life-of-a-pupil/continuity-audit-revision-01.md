## Internal continuity

- The opening's later-evidence limitation governs the representative rhythm
  and is renewed only when the evidence class changes.
- The chapter moves through a coherent day-and-year pattern: House, lessons,
  meals, work, leisure, outside contact, holidays, rules, environmental strain
  and synthesis.
- Age develops consistently from shared younger routine to third-year
  Hogsmeade access and sixth-year flexibility, always paired with permission or
  responsibility.
- Common-room privacy and girls' dormitory access do not conflict: one concerns
  limited privacy within a shared room, the other a specific gendered access
  rule.
- Public House identity at meals and matches is consistent with reduced-house
  intimacy during a sparsely attended Christmas.
- Friendship as support and friendship as a vehicle for cruelty are presented
  as distinct possibilities, not contradictory universal claims.
- Ordinary punishment, exceptional Forest work and Umbridge's abuse remain
  separate categories.
- The concluding phrase “disciplined independence” preserves both supervision
  and substantial pupil-managed time.
