## Central argument

Hogwarts was school, home and social world at once. Lessons, meals, rules and
House residence fixed the main obligations of a pupil's day, but much of life
between those anchors was organised by pupils themselves. Houses and prefects
distributed community and supervision through the school; age brought wider
privileges alongside heavier work and responsibility. The resulting condition
was disciplined independence rather than either constant management or neglect.

## I. The House at Either End of the Day — paragraphs 1–4

  not an invented schedule or timeless composite pupil.

- Source-access: signal at once that most detail comes from the 1990s.

- Present the observed later Gryffindor arrangement: year-group dormitory,

- Source-access: do not generalise five beds or Gryffindor design to all Houses.
