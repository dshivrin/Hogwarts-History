## Voice and historical writing

- In-universe historical-nonfiction voice: pass. Concrete events lead to
  institutional consequence; metadata carries research mechanics.
- Moderately formal British prose: pass. Spelling, titles and measured syntax
  match the current edition style.
- Knowledgeable but non-omniscient narrator: pass. Unknown origins, missing
  procedures and source limits are preserved without making the narrative
  evasive.
- Later editorial additions: pass. The material is integrated into a unified
  narrative while later histories, memories and post-1984 observation remain
  identifiable.
- Anecdotes serve institutional history: pass. Riddle and Lily show the school's
  reach; Hagrid's expulsion shows public consequence; Hagrid's war testimony
  shows reputation. None becomes a biographical diversion.

## Avoidance checks

- Muggle-facing exposition or explanation of familiar magical terms: none.
- Fan-wiki or lore-list presentation: none.
- Modern fandom or academic-journal language in reader-facing prose: none.
- Fake archaism or excessive drama: none.
- First-person authorial self-reference: none after compression; “the evidence”
  is used only where a historical limit must be stated.
- Repetitive confidence formulae: controlled. Qualifications occur at the point
  where they prevent a specific overclaim rather than as freestanding warnings.

## Repetition and compression

- Adjacent restatement: none remaining. Each evidentiary paragraph adds a new
  institutional function or a necessary source limit.
- Repeated caveats: reduced to the minimum needed to prevent the four principal
  errors—origin from recurrence, policy from a case, jurisdiction from pressure
  and defence measures from reputation.
- Conceptual repetition across sections: none. Transport addresses inheritance;
  admission addresses reach; offices address continuity; 1943 addresses public
  consequence; war addresses social significance; technology addresses
  material causation.
- Cross-chapter callbacks: reduced to the facts needed for the new longitudinal
  argument. Route detail, examination procedure, defence inventories,
  headmasterial powers and biographies are absent.
- Compression changes completed: later-source signalling was folded into
  narrative openings; the authorial phrase “permits us to go” was removed; the
  technology contrast was tightened around the two different causal mechanisms;
