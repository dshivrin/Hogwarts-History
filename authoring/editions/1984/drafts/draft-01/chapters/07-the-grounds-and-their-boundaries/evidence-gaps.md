# Chapter 7 Evidence Gaps — Revision 01

## Missing chronology

- No source dates the origin of the first-year boat crossing, links it to the
  founders, or establishes when the underground harbour was made.
- No source dates the first use of “Forbidden Forest” as an institutional name
  or the first rule restricting pupil entry.
- The origins of the lake, greenhouses, vegetable patch, gamekeeper’s dwelling,
  Quidditch pitch, gates, walls and carriage road are not established.
- Centaur residence in the Forest is undated. Merpeople residence in the lake is
  likewise undated.
- The thestral herd is established in the Harry era, but the beginning of its
  school maintenance and carriage service is not dated.
- The Acromantula colony can be traced to Hagrid’s school years and Aragog’s
  relocation, but the evidence does not establish school authorization.
- The Whomping Willow was planted in Remus Lupin’s arrival year; the selected
  passage does not itself give that calendar year.
- The anti-Apparition protection is asserted retrospectively to be longstanding,
  but its maker and first imposition remain unknown.

## Spatial limits

- Exact acreage, lake dimensions, Forest depth, road lengths and distances
  between landmarks are not established.
- No admissible source supplies a complete orientation of lake, Forest, gates,
  greenhouses, pitch and hut relative to one another.
- “High on the cliff over the lake” and the first-year tunnel establish vertical
  relationships, not a reliable plan or compass bearing.
- The school boundary is operationally visible at the gates for Apparition, but
  the gates are not proven to be the entire legal or magical perimeter.
- The extent to which the Forest lies inside, beside, or partly beyond property
  formally owned by Hogwarts is not established.

## Knowledge-access gaps

- Harry-era observations establish mature conditions but not their presence in
  1984.
- A04’s claim that castle and grounds have “always” had Anti-Apparition Charms is
  an official retrospective, not wording available to Bagshot.
- A09’s “nature reserve” formulation is a 2015 authorial description and does
  not establish a pre-1984 legal designation or management regime.
- The detailed lake settlement, centaur territorial confrontations and
  Acromantula access changes are later observations.
- *Cursed Child* evidence is post-1984 and must not be retrojected.

## Contradictions and ambiguities

- Later official material calls the water the “Great Lake”; narrative sources
  commonly say “the lake” or describe a “black lake.” The evidence does not
  establish that “Black Lake” was a formal pre-1984 proper name.
- “Forbidden Forest” and “forest” are both used. The corpus does not date the
  formal designation.
- Dumbledore later speaks casually of “our” centaurs, while centaurs assert
  separate law, reject service and claim the Forest as theirs. The possessive
  cannot safely establish school ownership.
- The school can restrict pupil access and send staff into the Forest without
  thereby demonstrating control over every resident or the whole territory.
- The giant squid is called semi-domesticated in a later official source; the
  legal, custodial and historical meaning of that description is unspecified.

## Unsupported assumptions to exclude

- founder-designed landscaping, founder-era boat tradition or founder-built
  harbour;
- exact fan-map geography, canonical compass directions or distances;
- school ownership of centaurs, merpeople, unicorns or Acromantulas;
- every dangerous Forest inhabitant being Dark, school-maintained, or permanent;
- all modern protections being ancient;
- exact Hogwarts pitch dimensions borrowed from general Quidditch standards;
- a “boathouse” unless used only as a cautious editorial synonym; the source
  directly establishes an underground harbour.

## Open questions

1. Was the first-year route created with the castle or adopted later?
2. Did Bagshot have documentary access to the Forest restriction or centaur
   presence before 1984?
3. What legal boundary, if any, separated Hogwarts land from centaur territory?
4. Were the merpeople consulted routinely, or only for exceptional operations?
5. Who built and maintained the greenhouses, harbour, gates and pitch?
6. Was the gamekeeper’s dwelling replaced or altered across different holders
   of the office?
7. When did the thestral carriage service begin?
8. Which parts of the landscape are natural, constructed, enchanted, or a
   mixture? The evidence resolves only individual examples.
9. Was the tournament maze fully removed immediately, and how was the pitch
   restored?
10. What, precisely, did post-Battle recognition of centaur land change? The
    later testimony supplies no instrument, grantor or boundaries.

## Research-layer issues discovered

- No canonical evidence defect was found. Several extraction-era titles use
  “Black Lake” as a normalized topic label even where the source note establishes
  only “the lake”; the chapter must follow inspected source wording rather than
  treat index labels as quotations.
