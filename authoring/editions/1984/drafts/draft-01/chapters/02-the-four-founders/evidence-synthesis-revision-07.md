## Chapter Purpose

Introduce the founders as four historical actors joined by a common
educational undertaking, distinguish the small documentary minimum from the
larger body of founder tradition, and explain how differing preferences for
pupils became a consequential disagreement about access to education. The
chapter stops before construction, the mature House system, Slytherin's
departure, and the Chamber narrative.
