## Repetition and compression

### Sentence and paragraph repetition

- The first-year list recurs for distinct functions: material preparation,
  curricular breadth, and the educational implications of tools.
- O.W.L.s develop through distinct stages: pre-1984 continuity, later
  administration, physical procedure, results, and course consequences.
- Teaching examples do not repeat subject descriptions; each adds a different
  method.
- Historical continuity and historical change receive separate paragraphs and
  are not collapsed into a false single narrative.
- The conclusion synthesises consequences without re-listing every subject or
  examination scene.

### Chapter-level conceptual repetition

- The eight outline movements answer separate questions: entry, common study,
  elective choice, teaching, assessment, advanced progression, historical
  development, and synthesis.
- Later-evidence qualifications occur beside the claims they govern rather
  than in repeated general disclaimer paragraphs.
- The detailed Book-and-Quill explanation remains in Chapter 3; Chapter 9 uses
  only the distinction between selection and notification.

### Compression and flow

- Reader-facing prose contains 2,438 words before hidden evidence metadata.
- No arbitrary length target was used.
- Subject names are grouped by educational function rather than expanded into
  an encyclopedia.
- Teacher and pupil incident detail was reduced to what reveals an educational
  mechanism.
- Concrete distinctions among ordinary exams, O.W.L.s, results, career advice,
  thresholds and advanced work were preserved because further compression
  would erase the progression argument.
