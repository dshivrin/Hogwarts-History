## Central argument

The later documented Hogwarts education moved pupils through a common magical
foundation towards elective and advanced study, combining books and theory with
supervised practical skill. Ordinary examinations measured progress, while
O.W.L. results increasingly determined N.E.W.T. access and occupational
possibility. Earlier evidence establishes only particular continuities and
changes, not a complete historical curriculum.
