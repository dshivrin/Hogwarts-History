## Chapter Purpose

The chapter begins at Chapter 11's boundary between a written record and a
responsive remainder. It distinguishes ghosts, ordinary portraits, trained
headmaster portraits, the Sorting Hat, and preserved memories by what each can
retain, how each can answer, and what each loses. It remains a chapter about
institutional memory rather than a catalogue of supernatural beings or a
general account of death.

## Executive finding

The proposed scope is supported, chiefly in the later unified-edition layer.
The evidence does not disclose one Hogwarts theory of memory. Instead, it
supports several mechanisms with different relations to a past person or
event. A ghost speaks as a surviving individual witness but remains selective
and limited. An ordinary portrait is a constructed representation; a trained
head portrait may preserve deliberately imparted knowledge for successors.
The Sorting Hat carries founder-associated judgement by tradition without
becoming a portrait or record. A Pensieve permits revisitation of preserved
memory, yet acquisition, alteration, witness interest, and interpretation
remain evidentiary limits.

The 1984 reconstruction is necessarily modest. Former-head portraits can be
dated to Dippet's office through a later-accessed 1940s scene, the Hat can be
shown operating in a 1971 memory, and the accepted style lock permits the
millennium-scale annual-song tradition. Most portrait functions, ghost customs,
Hat warnings, and Pensieve practice are observed or explained after 1984 and
must remain visibly later. The strongest institutional claim—the school-owned
Pensieve as a reference library of memories left by successive heads—comes
from a 2015 official retrospective source.

The two prior source-access blockers are closed. `ext-a17-005` now canonically
supports re-liveable memory, subconscious detail, second-party entry, and
movement within a memory. `beedle-ch04-007` now canonically supports the later
editorial distinction among moving photographs, speaking portraits, and
ghosts. Neither entry resolves chronology, consciousness, identity, complete
accuracy, or general Hogwarts access.
