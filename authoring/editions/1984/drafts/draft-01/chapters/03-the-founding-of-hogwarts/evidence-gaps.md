# Chapter 3 Evidence Gaps

## Unresolved questions

- The precise foundation year, season, and duration of construction are not recorded.
- No reviewed source identifies who proposed Hogwarts, chose the site, owned the land, designed the castle, financed it, or performed the work.
- The original castle plan and the age of its familiar towers, rooms, lake approaches, forest boundary, and internal enchantments are unknown.
- The first pupils' identities, ages, number, origin, journeys, residence, timetable, curriculum, and length of study are unknown.
- No source identifies a first Headmaster or establishes a founder-era governance structure beyond four founders working together and one founder-appointed caretaker.
- The original date of the Muggle-facing ruin enchantment is unknown.
- The reviewed evidence does not show that Hogwarts was Unplottable.
- Anti-Apparition protections are established only for the modern school; their origin is unknown.
- The maker and exact operation of the Book of Admittance and Quill of Acceptance are not recorded.
- The precise date at which the Sorting Hat was enchanted is unknown.

| Exact foundation date | `cos-ch09-003` says more than a thousand years ago and expressly leaves the date uncertain. | `gof-ch12-002` gives the same broad millennium-scale tradition. | The sources corroborate only an approximation. | Reject tenth-century precision and all invented years. |

## Rejected claims

- A precise tenth-century founding date or named foundation year.
- A founder-era Scottish Highlands site described from modern scenery.
- Named architects, builders, craftspeople, creatures, construction spells, ceremonies, foundation stones, wings, or building phases.
- A founder-era seven-year course, modern timetable, curriculum, invitation-letter system, or school bureaucracy.
- Hogwarts as explicitly Unplottable on the reviewed evidence.
- Founder-created anti-Apparition barriers or later security systems.
- The Pensieve legend as proof of why the site was chosen.
- Hankerton Humble as the first caretaker.
- Any claim that the founders personally performed all physical construction.
