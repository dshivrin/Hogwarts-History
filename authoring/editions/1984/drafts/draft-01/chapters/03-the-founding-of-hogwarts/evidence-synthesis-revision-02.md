## Chapter Purpose

Reconstruct the supported movement from a shared educational purpose to a
durable school: a remote castle, pupils actively sought and taught, and limited
institutional mechanisms capable of continuing beyond the founders. The
chapter ends while all four founders still operate Hogwarts and leaves the
House system, rupture, detailed castle history, mature governance, curriculum,
and pupil life to their assigned chapters.
