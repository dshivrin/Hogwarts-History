## Central argument

Hogwarts was governed through overlapping authorities rather than by a single
sovereign office. Headmaster, staff, governors, and Ministry possessed distinct
but incompletely documented powers. Their limits appear most clearly in
conflict, especially in later interventions that must not be backdated.
