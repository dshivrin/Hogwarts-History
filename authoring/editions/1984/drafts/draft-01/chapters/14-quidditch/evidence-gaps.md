# Chapter 14 evidence gaps

## G14-01 — Origin of Quidditch at Hogwarts

**Status:** origin unknown  
**Material effect:** prevents any claim that Quidditch belonged to Hogwarts from its foundation, or any exact adoption date.

General Quidditch chronology establishes that several features of the mature game postdate Hogwarts. The indexed Hogwarts-specific evidence does not identify when broom sport, Quidditch, House teams, the Quidditch Cup, or the pitch first appeared at the school.

## G14-02 — Earlier Hogwarts Quidditch claims in official source A12 are not extracted

**Status:** source-verification/extraction gap; deferable, not chapter-blocking  
**Material effect:** potentially changes the earliest securely indexed evidence for a Hogwarts team and for a Cup-deciding school match.

The repository’s official Rowling snapshot `resources/external/official-rowling/harrypotter-com/a12-professor-mcgonagall.md` contains statements that Isobel Ross was captain of a school Quidditch team and that Minerva McGonagall played in a Gryffindor–Slytherin match deciding the Quidditch Cup. Existing evidence entries `ext-a12-001` through `ext-a12-006` do not extract those claims. Neighbouring IDs cannot be used as substitutes.

Until the research layer creates dedicated evidence entries, the chapter must not cite or rely on these facts. The current outline therefore treats `dh-ch10-004` as the earliest securely indexed House-team evidence and `ext-a32-003` as the earliest securely indexed dedicated-pitch evidence, without claiming that either marks an origin.

## G14-03 — Origins of House teams and the Hogwarts Quidditch Cup

**Status:** origin unknown  
**Material effect:** requires the chapter to distinguish the observed mature four-team system from its undocumented beginnings.

The later novels securely show teams representing all four Houses and a season culminating in a Quidditch Cup. No selected indexed evidence gives the creation date of the teams, the first Cup, or an uninterrupted institutional succession.

## G14-04 — Construction and alteration of the Hogwarts pitch

**Status:** origin unknown  
**Material effect:** prevents architectural chronology.

The evidence establishes a dedicated pitch before 1984 and later observes stands, changing rooms, a captain’s office, storage, and a commentary arrangement. It does not date construction, prove that all facilities are coeval, or establish when Hogwarts adopted general changes such as fixed hoops.

## G14-05 — Complete fixture formula

**Status:** not established  
**Material effect:** prevents presenting a formal round-robin schedule as stated fact.

Observed seasons show matches among the four House teams, cumulative scores and margins affecting Cup prospects, and a final match resolving the Cup. The selected evidence does not itself state a complete tournament rule, tie-break procedure, or universal annual fixture order. The chapter may describe only what the observations support.

## G14-06 — Universal selection and first-year rules

**Status:** not established  
**Material effect:** limits claims about trials and eligibility.

Later seasons show captains running trials and choosing replacements, while staff communicate opportunities and sometimes intervene. The supply list prohibits first-years from bringing personal broomsticks; it does not state a universal ban on first-years playing. Harry’s recruitment is expressly exceptional and cannot supply a general rule.

## G14-07 — Professional-player pipeline

**Status:** weakly evidenced  
**Material effect:** excludes a proposed catalogue of professional alumni.

`hbp-ch07-006` associates Gwenog Jones with Slughorn’s former-pupil network, but does not establish that she played for a Hogwarts House team. No person should be presented as a former Hogwarts player who became professional without evidence for both parts of that claim.

## G14-08 — Old Quidditch trophies, plaques, and record books

**Status:** no specific indexed evidence located  
**Material effect:** narrows the memory section to photographs, retrospective match memory, captaincy, Cup presentation, and recurring House expectations.

The generic trophy-room evidence does not identify a Quidditch trophy or establish a dated team practice. No origin claim can be built from it.
