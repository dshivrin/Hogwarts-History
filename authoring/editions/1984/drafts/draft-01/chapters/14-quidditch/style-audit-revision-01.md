## Compression and narrative-flow audit

- Movement 3 occupies only paragraphs 4 and 5. It establishes the Snitch,
  pitch, rule, hoop and broom chronology needed for the Hogwarts argument and
  omits general administration, detailed fouls, match termination rules,
  international tournament narrative and professional officiating.
- The temporal pivot from mature Hogwarts importance to older general history
  occurs in paragraph 3; the return to Hogwarts occurs explicitly in paragraph
  6.
- The two pre-1984 traces lead directly into the later-observed mature system;
  the gap between them is stated rather than filled.
- The team sequence moves from structure to positions, captaincy, trials,
  training and equipment without repeating the full roster.
- The pitch sequence moves from facilities to repurposing and then to the
  assembled match-day audience.
- Match day moves from audience to support, operation, commentary and
  cross-House attention before the season broadens the time scale.
- The risk sequence distinguishes designed danger, regulation, injury and
  external breach.
- Interrupted seasons follow ordinary season structure, so exceptions reveal
  rather than pre-empt the normal institution.
- The conclusion completes the Hogwarts argument before its last sentences
  turn towards the wider wizarding world.

## Repetition and diction audit

- Automated comparison found no repeated ten-word sequence within the draft.
- Automated comparison found no shared eight- or ten-word sequence with the
  recorded Chapter 4, 6, 8 and 13 baselines.
- Repeated terms such as *House*, *pitch*, *season*, *Cup* and *institution*
  are necessary subject terms; their functions change across organisation,
  space, time, reward and synthesis.
- The opening and conclusion both join House identity to public competition,
  but the conclusion earns the recurrence after establishing teams, pitch,
  season, spectatorship, risk and memory.
- The prose avoids a catalogue of famous matches or players and does not
  reproduce a rules manual.
- Limitations appear where the evidentiary boundary changes rather than in a
  repeated generic disclaimer.
