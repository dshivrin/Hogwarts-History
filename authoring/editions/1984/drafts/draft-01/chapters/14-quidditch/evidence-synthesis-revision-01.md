# Chapter 14 evidence synthesis — revision 01

## Verdict

**PASS for outline.**

The evidence supports a chapter about Quidditch as a mature Hogwarts institution while requiring a restrained account of its beginnings. It does not support an origin date, uninterrupted institutional continuity from the founders, or the backward projection of 1990s procedures.

The unresolved A12 extraction issue is **deferable** rather than blocking: the proposed chapter can truthfully state that the origin is unknown and use the securely indexed pre-1984 photograph and pitch evidence. If dedicated A12 entries are later created, the early-history movement should be re-audited before drafting or approval.

## Consolidated claims

### C14-01 — Hogwarts predates the mature game

**Disposition:** advance to outline  
**Claim:** Hogwarts was already old before many defining features of modern Quidditch appeared. Early broom flight and Queerditch-like play do not justify placing modern Quidditch at the school’s foundation.

**Evidence:** `cos-ch09-003`, `qtta-ch01-002`, `qtta-ch02-001`, `qtta-ch03-001`, `qtta-ch03-002`, `qtta-ch04-001`, `qtta-ch04-002`, `qtta-ch04-003`

**Qualification:** Hogwarts’s founding year and the dates of some intermediate sporting developments are approximate. The evidence establishes chronological incompatibility with a fully mature founders-era game, not the date of school adoption.

### C14-02 — The familiar game was assembled and regulated over centuries

**Disposition:** advance in compressed form  
**Claim:** Permanent pitches, secrecy measures, standard playing areas, fixed hoops, nationally agreed rules, specialised roles, organised British competition, and sporting brooms arose at different dates. Hogwarts could only have adopted the mature institution through later adaptation.

**Evidence:** `qtta-ch05-001`, `qtta-ch05-002`, `qtta-ch06-001`, `qtta-ch06-003`, `qtta-ch06-005`, `qtta-ch06-006`, `qtta-ch06-007`, `qtta-ch06-008`, `qtta-ch06-010`, `qtta-ch07-001`, `qtta-ch08-001`, `qtta-ch09-001`, `qtta-ch09-003`, `qtta-ch09-006`

**Qualification:** These entries describe Quidditch generally. They do not date Hogwarts adoption, prove direct departmental control of school matches, or establish that every professional rule governed the school.

### C14-03 — The earliest securely indexed Hogwarts evidence is pre-1984 but not ancient

**Disposition:** advance to outline  
**Claim:** Existing indexed evidence securely establishes a dedicated Hogwarts Quidditch pitch during Lockhart’s school years and a Slytherin House team, with Regulus Black as Seeker, before 1984.

**Evidence:** `ext-a32-003`, `dh-ch10-004`

**Qualification:** Both facts are carried by later sources and neither marks an origin. A relevant official McGonagall source is present in the repository but lacks dedicated extracted evidence; see V14-01.

### C14-04 — The mature school system joined House teams to the seven-player game

**Disposition:** advance to outline  
**Claim:** Later observations show the four Houses fielding teams in the familiar seven-player arrangement, with captains and role specialisation. This is secure for the observed mature institution.

**Evidence:** `ps-ch09-003`, `cos-ch07-001`, `poa-ch08-002`, `gof-ch06-004`, `poa-ch15-006`, `hbp-ch24-007`

**Qualification:** The evidence does not show when each House first formed a team or prove that the same roster structure existed in every earlier period.

### C14-05 — Captaincy organised selection, preparation, and team continuity

**Disposition:** advance to outline  
**Claim:** In later observed seasons, captains convened teams, scheduled or conducted trials, judged applicants, organised practice, and chose replacements. Captaincy also carried recognised status.

**Evidence:** `cos-ch07-002`, `poa-ch08-002`, `ootp-ch13-007`, `ootp-ch14-004`, `hbp-ch06-001`, `hbp-ch08-004`, `hbp-ch09-002`, `hbp-ch11-003`, `hbp-ch14-003`, `hbp-ch14-004`

**Qualification:** Procedures vary by House and year. Heads of House communicate or intervene, but the evidence does not establish a universal written selection code or appointment authority for captains.

### C14-06 — Equipment provision mixed school resources, personal ownership, and patronage

**Disposition:** advance to outline  
**Claim:** Hogwarts supplied brooms for instruction and later trials, while players could own superior brooms and an entire team could receive donated equipment. The first-year personal-broom restriction is distinct from playing eligibility.

**Evidence:** `ps-ch05-002`, `ps-ch09-002`, `ps-ch09-003`, `cos-ch07-002`, `hbp-ch11-003`

**Qualification:** School lesson brooms are not proved to be standard match equipment. Harry’s first-year recruitment and broom acquisition are exceptional.

### C14-07 — The pitch made the institution permanent in space

**Disposition:** advance to outline  
**Claim:** A dedicated pitch, raised stands, changing facilities, equipment storage, and later a captain’s office supported training, matches, spectatorship, and administration. Its repurposing for the Triwizard maze confirms that it was a large, established school arena.

**Evidence:** `ext-a32-003`, `ps-ch11-004`, `cos-ch10-004`, `ootp-ch14-004`, `gof-ch28-004`, `gof-ch31-002`

**Qualification:** The construction date, original plan, maintenance regime, and dates of later facilities are unknown. General QTA pitch standards cannot fill those gaps.

### C14-08 — Match day converted team play into collective spectatorship

**Disposition:** advance to outline  
**Claim:** Whole-school attendance, House colours, banners, supporter objects and songs, formal starts, refereeing, commentary, staff supervision, and post-match celebration made the match a schoolwide event rather than the private recreation of fourteen players.

**Evidence:** `ps-ch11-004`, `ps-ch11-005`, `cos-ch10-003`, `poa-ch15-006`, `poa-ch15-007`, `ootp-ch19-005`, `ootp-ch19-006`, `ootp-ch30-003`, `hbp-ch14-005`

**Qualification:** These are later observations. Not every match is shown with identical commentary, attendance, or staff presence; the 1994 Cup decider and 1995 harassment are unusually intense.

### C14-09 — The Cup gave House rivalry a season and cumulative stakes

**Disposition:** advance to outline  
**Claim:** Later seasons show autumn-to-spring recurrence, practice shaped by standings, scoring margins affecting Cup prospects, and a final match resolving the trophy. This supports a seasonal institution, but not a fully stated tournament rulebook.

**Evidence:** `ps-ch11-001`, `poa-ch12-004`, `poa-ch15-004`, `poa-ch15-007`, `ootp-ch19-003`, `ootp-ch30-003`, `hbp-ch24-007`

**Qualification:** The complete fixture formula, tie-breakers, and universal order of matches are not stated. The evidence supports cumulative scoring significance, not an invented clean bracket or confidently stated round robin.

### C14-10 — Hogwarts accepted substantial risk within supervised sport

**Disposition:** advance to outline  
**Claim:** Later evidence shows hard practices, Bludger danger, fouls, falls, collision injuries, bad weather, and hospital-wing consequences alongside refereeing, equipment custody, medical treatment, staff intervention, and exceptional protective measures.

**Evidence:** `cos-ch10-004`, `cos-ch10-005`, `poa-ch09-006`, `poa-ch09-007`, `poa-ch15-005`, `ootp-ch19-006`, `hbp-ch14-004`, `hbp-ch19-005`

**Qualification:** Sabotage, Dementors, unprecedented security, and post-whistle violence must not be described as ordinary accepted practice. The evidence supports institutional tolerance of considerable sporting risk, not indifference to every danger.

### C14-11 — Interruptions reveal the expected rhythm of the institution

**Disposition:** advance to outline  
**Claim:** Emergency cancellation, a full Triwizard-year cancellation, pitch repurposing, later player bans, and replacement recruitment were explicitly noticed because they interrupted an established expectation of House competition.

**Evidence:** `cos-ch14-003`, `gof-ch12-005`, `gof-ch28-004`, `gof-ch31-002`, `ootp-ch19-003`, `ootp-ch19-007`, `ootp-ch21-005`

**Qualification:** All are post-1984 events and temporary disruptions. They reveal the mature normal by contrast but cannot be inserted into a 1984 narrator’s direct historical knowledge.

### C14-12 — Membership and memory extended beyond an individual fixture

**Disposition:** advance to outline  
**Claim:** Team photographs, captain’s badges, remembered captains and results, inherited Cup expectations, and celebrations inside the castle gave players and teams a durable place in House memory.

**Evidence:** `dh-ch10-004`, `gof-ch06-004`, `poa-ch15-004`, `poa-ch15-007`, `ootp-ch30-003`, `hbp-ch06-001`, `hbp-ch24-007`

**Qualification:** No specific old Quidditch plaque, dated record book, or founding trophy evidence was located. Later memories do not establish the origin of the practice remembered.

### C14-13 — Hogwarts Quidditch was both local and part of a wider sporting culture

**Disposition:** advance only as closing bridge  
**Claim:** The school’s House competition was intensely local, but it used a sport with national and international institutions beyond the grounds. That double character can provide a restrained transition to Chapter 15.

**Evidence:** `qtta-ch07-001`, `qtta-ch08-001`, together with the Hogwarts-specific evidence under C14-08 and C14-09

**Qualification:** No formal relationship between Hogwarts and the League, European Cup, or World Cup is established here. The bridge must remain conceptual and brief.

## Central-argument decision

The proposed argument survives with one important correction. The evidence supports saying that Hogwarts did not create Quidditch, that the school predates the mature sport, and that the game became exceptionally well suited to the House structure. It does **not** establish the adoption event itself or continuous practice from a particular century. The chapter should therefore make adaptation an inference from chronology and mature institutional fit, not a narrated origin story.
