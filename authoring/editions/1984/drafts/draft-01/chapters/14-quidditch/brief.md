# Chapter 14 — Quidditch

## Editorial purpose

Explain how Quidditch became a Hogwarts institution: a recurring House competition that joined teams, spectators, a permanent playing space, seasonal standings, achievement, risk, and inherited memory.

The chapter is not a general history of Quidditch or a rules manual. General history is included only where it establishes which parts of the mature game postdate Hogwarts and therefore cannot safely be assigned to the founders or earliest pupils.

## Chronological boundary

The reconstructed edition is bounded at 1984. Later observations may recover an older condition or illuminate the mature institution, but they must be identified as later-carried evidence or later observation. Harry-era procedures are not to be projected into earlier centuries.

## Working argument

Hogwarts predates the mature form of Quidditch, and the date at which the school adopted the game is unknown. Securely indexed evidence nevertheless places a dedicated pitch and a House team at Hogwarts before 1984. Later observations show why the institution endured: the four Houses supplied durable teams and loyalties; the grounds supplied a common arena; the school year supplied recurrence; and spectatorship, commentary, standings, and the Quidditch Cup made each contest a public expression of House identity.

This argument is deliberately narrower than a claim of continuous practice from the school’s foundation.

## Scope boundaries

- Chapter 4 owns the House system. Chapter 14 uses Houses as sporting units.
- Chapters 6 and 7 own the castle and grounds. Chapter 14 treats the pitch only as sporting infrastructure.
- Chapter 8 owns governance. Chapter 14 includes authority only where it enables, regulates, or interrupts play.
- Chapter 13 owns the general account of custom and ceremony. Chapter 14 demonstrates the seasonal institution in depth.
- Chapter 15 is *Hogwarts and the Wider Wizarding World*. The final transition may note that Hogwarts’s intensely local competition used a sport shared across the wider wizarding world.
