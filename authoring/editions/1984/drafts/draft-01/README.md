# Draft 01

Draft 01 is the first full style experiment for the reconstructed 1984 edition.
Chapters will be prepared and produced one at a time, with the early chapters
used to calibrate the book-wide voice.

Chapters 1–3 are expected to produce revisions to the initial style guide. A
substantially different voice or editorial approach should eventually become a
sibling draft such as `draft-02`, rather than silently replacing the entire
first attempt.

No chapter exists yet. The `chapters/` directory remains empty until a chapter
enters the preparation workflow.
