the	headmaster	was	here.”

spoke	politely,	but	the	fire	was	still	blazing	in	his	blue	eyes.

“Yeh	can’	take	Dumbledore!”	yelled	Hagrid,	making	Fang	the	boarhound

won’	stand	a	chance!	There’ll	be	killin’	next!”
