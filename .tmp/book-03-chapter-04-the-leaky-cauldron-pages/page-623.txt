fifth	year	at	Hogwarts;	the	newly	elected	Head	Boy,	Percy;	and	the	Weasleys’
youngest	child	and	only	girl,	Ginny.

solemnly	as	though	he	and	Harry	had	never	met	and	said,	“Harry.	How	nice	to
see	you.”
