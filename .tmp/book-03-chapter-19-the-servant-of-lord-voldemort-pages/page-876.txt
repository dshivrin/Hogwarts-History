But	there	was	a	mad	glint	in	Snape’s	eyes	that	Harry	had	never	seen	before.
He	seemed	beyond	reason.
“Come	on,	all	of	you,”	he	said.	He	clicked	his	fingers,	and	the	ends	of	the
cords	that	bound	Lupin	flew	to	his	hands.	“I’ll	drag	the	werewolf.	Perhaps	the
dementors	will	have	a	Kiss	for	him	too	—”
Before	he	knew	what	he	was	doing,	Harry	had	crossed	the	room	in	three
strides	and	blocked	the	door.
“Get	out	of	the	way,	Potter,	you’re	in	enough	trouble	already,”	snarled
Snape.	“If	I	hadn’t	been	here	to	save	your	skin	—”
“Professor	Lupin	could	have	killed	me	about	a	hundred	times	this	year,”
Harry	said.	“I’ve	been	alone	with	him	loads	of	times,	having	defense	lessons
against	the	dementors.	If	he	was	helping	Black,	why	didn’t	he	just	finish	me
off	then?”
“Don’t	ask	me	to	fathom	the	way	a	werewolf’s	mind	works,”	hissed	Snape.
“Get	out	of	the	way,	Potter.”
“YOU’RE	PATHETIC!”	Harry	yelled.	“JUST	BECAUSE	THEY	MADE	A
FOOL	OF	YOU	AT	SCHOOL	YOU	WON’T	EVEN	LISTEN	—”
