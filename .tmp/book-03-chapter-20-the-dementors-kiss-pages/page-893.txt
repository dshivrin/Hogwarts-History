“Oh,	my	—”	Hermione	gasped.	“He	didn’t	take	his	potion	tonight!	He’s	not

“Run,”	Sirius	whispered.	“Run.	Now.”
But	Harry	couldn’t	run.	Ron	was	chained	to	Pettigrew	and	Lupin.	He	leapt
forward	but	Sirius	caught	him	around	the	chest	and	threw	him	back.
“Leave	it	to	me	—	RUN!”
There	was	a	terrible	snarling	noise.	Lupin’s	head	was	lengthening.	So	was
his	body.	His	shoulders	were	hunching.	Hair	was	sprouting	visibly	on	his	face
and	hands,	which	were	curling	into	clawed	paws.	Crookshanks’s	hair	was	on
end	again;	he	was	backing	away	—
