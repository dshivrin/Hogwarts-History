“The	new	password’s	‘Fortuna	Major’!”
“Oh	 no,”	 said	 Neville	 Longbottom	 sadly.	 He	 always	 had	 trouble
remembering	the	passwords.
Through	the	portrait	hole	and	across	the	common	room,	the	girls	and	boys
divided	toward	their	separate	staircases.	Harry	climbed	the	spiral	stair	with	no
thought	in	his	head	except	how	glad	he	was	to	be	back.	They	reached	their
familiar,	circular	dormitory	with	its	five	four-poster	beds,	and	Harry,	looking
around,	felt	he	was	home	at	last.
